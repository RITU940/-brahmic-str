\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Absolute accuracy.} Our macro-average (\wrrBmean\%) clears the off-the-shelf OCR floor
(\tessMean\%), though the weakest script does not: Tamil (\wrrBtamil\%) falls below it, and all
nine sit far under the supervised ceiling ($\parseqFinetuned\%$ with real labels~\cite{bstd}). The
synthetic$\rightarrow$real domain gap is the dominant residual: training loss reaches ${\sim}0.06$
while real-image WRR stays modest. \textbf{The target script must be known.} $\pi^{-1}$ resolves a
pivot string into one script, so the protocol assumes script identity is fixed before decoding.
That is given for a held-out-script benchmark, and an extra component for mixed-script scenes.
\textbf{One family, and a located boundary.} Nine scripts, one benchmark, one family whose shared
structure the pivot exploits by design; Khmer (Sec.~\ref{sec:khmer}) shows the method
\emph{failing} outside it (\khmerB\% WRR). \textbf{One architecture.} All transfer results use
Florence-2; a from-scratch CRNN replicates the direction at ${\sim}\crnnRatioMean$ of the
magnitude (Sec.~\ref{sec:crnn}), so backbone-independence of the effect size is untested.
\textbf{Model imperfections.} Gujarati underperforms its coverage, one Rung-A baseline breaches
the predicted $<5$ line (Devanagari, \wrrAdevanagari), and the final-script forecast
under-called by $\gurmukhiSigma\sigma$; all reported, none explained away. \textbf{Compute.} One
24\,GB GPU; budgets were fixed by preregistration, not tuned.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built an evaluation protocol for a condition existing benchmarks do not score, a script with
no real labeled image at all, and ran a forecasting campaign inside it. Under it, a small open
VLM fine-tuned in a shared grapheme pivot space on rendered synthetic text alone reads real
photographs of all nine unseen Brahmic scripts at \wrrBmin--\wrrBmax\% word accuracy, and a
stock-BPE ablation collapses that transfer, isolating output-space structure as the mechanism. The
forecasting record is mostly one of misses: primary hypothesis falsified, scaling magnitude
missed by $\scaleSlopeRatio\times$, coverage failing out-of-sample, the one out-of-block call
(Khmer) missed by $\khmerResidRMSE\sigma$. We advance no predictive law. Every call ships in the
supplement with its receipt, and the failures locate the method's boundary rather than leave it
for someone else to find.
