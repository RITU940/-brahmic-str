\begin{abstract}
Scene-text recognition (STR) is effectively solved for Latin script yet fails for most of the
world's writing systems, and no benchmark measures the case that matters for the long tail:
reading a script for which \emph{no real labeled image exists at all}. We contribute an
\emph{evaluation protocol} for exactly this setting and a \emph{predictive instrument} for it, and
we stress-test both --- including against our own hypotheses. The protocol is leave-one-script-out
over nine Brahmic scripts of a public real-image benchmark: the held-out script is seen only as
rendered synthetic images, never a real photograph, and is read in a shared grapheme \emph{pivot}
space; we add an out-of-benchmark probe on Khmer (a disjoint Unicode block outside the benchmark).
Every forecast in the campaign was filed \emph{prospectively} --- committed and pushed before the
run it predicts --- and is scored here including its misses.

Under the protocol, a compact open vision--language model reads every held-out script
(\wrrBmin--\wrrBmax\% word recognition, character accuracy up to \chaBbengali\%) from source-only
baselines of \wrrAtamil--\wrrAdevanagari\%. A prompted 7B frontier VLM, scored with every
generosity, reads only Bengali and Devanagari (\vlmWrrBengali/\vlmWrrDevanagari\%) and loses to the
compact model on \vlmScriptsWon\ of nine; we also report off-the-shelf and supervised-specialist
recognizers on the identical crops as an interpretable floor and ceiling. %TODO(anchors)
A controlled ablation isolates the pivot --- not fine-tuning per se --- as the transfer mechanism:
replacing it with the stock BPE tokenizer degrades all nine scripts
($\bpeRatioMin\times$--$\bpeRatioMax\times$; sign test $p=\bpeSignP$), on three despite
equal-or-better character accuracy. The evaluation is deliberately adversarial to our own claims:
the preregistered primary hypothesis (tokenizer fertility predicts transfer) is \emph{falsified}
(Spearman $\spearFert$); a prospectively filed dose--response is \emph{log-linear with a
script-invariant slope} ($\scaleSlope$ WRR/doubling) but the pre-committed magnitude missed by
$\scaleSlopeRatio\times$; pivot-space coverage does not generalize out-of-sample ($r=\covOOSr$); and
a from-scratch CRNN replicates the direction but not the magnitude. We report each as filed. The
protocol, the prospective-forecast record, and a machine-checked verification chain --- released in
full --- are the contribution; the pivot is the evidence.
\end{abstract}
